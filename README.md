# AcuteSemaPaper
Prelimbic cortex to nucleus accumbens signaling and semaglutide-induced attenuation of food seeking


This repository contains raw behavioral data associated with the manuscript:

**“Prelimbic cortex to nucleus accumbens signaling and semaglutide-induced attenuation of food seeking”**

Li et al., Matikainen-Ankney Lab, Rutgers University.

Data are organized by manuscript figure:
- **Fig 1:** Fixed Ratio 1 (FR1) responding following acute semaglutide administration.
- **Fig 2:** Reversal learning following acute semaglutide administration.
- **Fig 4:** Cumulative Effort Progressive Ratio (CEPR) responding following acute semaglutide administration.
- **Fig 5:** Effects of chemogenetic inhibition of prelimbic cortex projections to the nucleus accumbens (PL–NAc) on reversal learning.
- **Fig 6:** Effects of PL–NAc chemogenetic inhibition on semaglutide-induced suppression of food seeking.


Files contain raw behavioral data exported from FED operant conditioning systems in CSV format. Original filenames have been retained.

For experimental procedures, data analysis, sample sizes, and exclusion criteria, please refer to the manuscript Methods and figure legends, or contact bridget.m@rutgers.edu.
